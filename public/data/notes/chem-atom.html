Atomic Structure — short notes

Chemistry · General

Subatomic particles

Proton: mass 1 u, charge +1. Neutron: mass 1 u, no charge. Electron: ≈ 1/1836 u, charge −1.

Isotopes

Same Z, different A. Chlorine: Cl-35 (75%) and Cl-37 (25%) → RAM 35.5.

Configuration

1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d 4p … Cr and Cu are exceptions.
