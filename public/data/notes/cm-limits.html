Limits & Continuity — worked examples

Combined Mathematics · Pure · A/L

Standard limits

  • lim(x→0) sin x / x = 1
  • lim(x→0) tan x / x = 1
  • lim(x→0) (ex − 1) / x = 1
  • lim(x→a) (xn − an)/(x − a) = n an−1

Worked example

lim(x→0) (sin 3x)/x = lim 3 · (sin 3x)/(3x) = 3.

Continuity at x = a

  1. f(a) exists
  2. both one-sided limits exist and agree
  3. limit = f(a)
